Clarity to decide.

Yes — it is a Meridian specialty. Working from plans, project information and references, we bring façades, amenities, interiors and surroundings to life before the first brick is laid.

A 4K master, campaign cutdowns, a launch website or landing page, identity assets, music and sound design, plus organized source files for your team — the scope is agreed item by item.
